About Peter

I am a passionate event photographer based in New Jersey, dedicated to capturing the authentic emotion and energy that makes every occasion unique. With an eye trained on the decisive moment, I work unobtrusively so your guests feel at ease and your memories look effortless.

My approach blends photojournalistic candor with a refined aesthetic — resulting in images that are both timeless and deeply personal. Every gallery I deliver is carefully edited to reflect the mood, light, and spirit of your event.
